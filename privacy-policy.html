Privacy Policy

For Magpie Peaks, Boom Bloom, Tidewarden and Army Dash · Effective 9 October 2026

These games are made by Just Cool Games (“we”). This page explains what information the games and the services inside them use, and the choices you have. In short: the games don’t ask for your name or email, your progress stays on your phone (or in your own Play Games account, if you use one), and the free version shows ads from Google, which uses some information about your device to do so.

Your progress

Your progress, settings and unlocks are saved on your phone. We don’t receive them. Deleting the game deletes them from your phone. In Magpie Peaks, Tidewarden and Army Dash on Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also kept in your Play Games account (Saved Games), so it comes back when you install the game on another phone. Google keeps that copy under its own privacy policy; we don’t receive it.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between levels. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), and how you interact with ads. Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before any ad is shown. You can change your answer at any time in the game’s Settings → Privacy → Choices.
  • On iPhone, the game asks whether it may track you for ads. If you say no, ads are not personalised using your device’s advertising ID.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • In the games that offer it, buying Remove ads turns off the ads between levels.

Purchases

Army Dash has no purchases. In the other games, Remove ads is bought through Google Play or the App Store. They handle the payment; we only learn whether you own it, so the game can turn ads off.

Leaderboards and achievements

Magpie Peaks, Tidewarden and Army Dash can post your scores, and Magpie Peaks the achievements you earn, to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center if you are signed in to them. Your scores, achievements and the player name you use there are then handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

Play data

In Magpie Peaks, Tidewarden and Army Dash, if “Share play data” is on in Settings, the game sends Google Analytics for Firebase information about how you play: levels started and finished, scores, time played, the game’s frame rate, any game errors, when an ad break plays, whether you buy Remove ads, when the game asks you for a rating, and what you pick up and spend in a game (in Magpie Peaks the trinkets you buy and sell, in Tidewarden the upgrades you choose and the pearls you spend, in Army Dash the units you find and upgrade), with an identifier Firebase makes for the game on your phone. Where privacy law asks for consent first (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, or wherever Google’s consent check says so), the switch is off until you turn it on; elsewhere it is on until you turn it off. It doesn’t include your name, contact details or advertising ID. We use it only to balance and fix the games. Google handles it under Google’s privacy policy. Turning the switch off stops it.

Boom Bloom doesn’t send us any information about how you play.

How long it is kept

  • Your progress stays on your phone until you delete the game, or use Reset progress in Settings. The Play Games copy stays in your Play Games account until you delete it there (in the Google Play Games app, or in your Google account’s settings).
  • Google Analytics keeps play data for 2 months, then deletes it. Reports made from it (counts, with no identifier) stay with us. Turning Share play data off stops it at once; deleting the game removes the identifier from your phone. To have the play data from your phone deleted sooner, write to us.
  • Google keeps ad and purchase information as its privacy policy says.

Keeping it safe

Everything the games send goes over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Google’s services. We don’t run servers of our own that store it. Only we can see the play data, in Google’s Firebase console.

Where it is handled

Google (ads, Google Play, Google Play Games, Google Analytics for Firebase) and Apple (the App Store, Game Center) handle the information above on their own servers, which may be outside your country, under their privacy policies: Google’s and Apple’s.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK or some US states), you may have the right to see, correct or delete information about you, to object to its use or withdraw your consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we don’t receive your progress or any account details, most requests are answered by the choices above: change your ad choices in Settings → Privacy → Choices, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings, and manage what Google or Apple hold in your Google account or Apple ID. For anything else, write to us at the address below.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
