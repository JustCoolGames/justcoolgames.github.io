Privacy Policy

For Magpie Peaks, Boom Bloom and Tidewarden · Effective 30 September 2026

These games are made by Just Cool Games (“we”). This page explains what information the games and the services inside them use, and the choices you have. In short: the games don’t ask for your name or email, your progress stays on your phone, and the free version shows ads from Google, which uses some information about your device to do so.

Your progress

Your progress, settings and unlocks are saved on your phone only. We don’t receive them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between levels. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), and how you interact with ads. Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before any ad is shown. You can change your answer at any time in the game’s Settings under Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the game asks whether it may track you for ads. If you say no, ads are not personalised using your device’s advertising ID.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • Buying Remove ads turns off the ads between levels.

Purchases

Remove ads is bought through Google Play or the App Store. They handle the payment; we only learn whether you own it, so the game can turn ads off.

Leaderboards

Magpie Peaks can post your Endless streak to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center if you are signed in to them. Your score and the player name you use there are then handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

Play data

The games don’t send us any information about how you play.

Where it is handled

Google (ads, Google Play, Google Play Games) and Apple (the App Store, Game Center) handle the information above on their own servers, which may be outside your country, under their privacy policies: Google’s and Apple’s.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK or some US states), you may have the right to see, correct or delete information about you, to object to its use or withdraw your consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we don’t receive your progress or any account details, most requests are answered by the choices above: change your ad choices under Privacy choices in Settings, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings, and manage what Google or Apple hold in your Google account or Apple ID. For anything else, write to us at the address below.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
