Support

For Magpie Peaks, Boom Bloom, Tidewarden and Army Dash.

Write to us: JustCoolGamesSupport@gmail.com

Tell us which game, your phone and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

I bought Remove ads. How do I get it on another phone?

Sign in to the same Google or Apple account, open the game, then Settings → Restore purchase.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. We don’t keep a copy, so deleting the game deletes your progress, and it doesn’t move to a new phone on its own. In Army Dash, Settings → Save code gives you a code to copy, and Settings → Load a save code on the new phone brings your progress back.

I saw an ad that was wrong, or not right for the game.

Open Settings → Report an ad, or write to us with what the ad was for (Army Dash has no Report an ad button yet). The ads come from Google AdMob, which lets us block ads we don’t want.

How do I change what the ads can use?

Settings → Privacy choices (in Army Dash: Settings → Privacy → Choices). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks before the first ad. On iPhone, you can also change tracking in the iPhone’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Our privacy policy explains the rest.

Leaderboards

Magpie Peaks posts your Endless streak, and Army Dash your Daily Dash score and Endless distance, to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center when you are signed in to them. In Army Dash, open the Daily Dash or Endless board and tap World ranking to see where you stand. Your name there is the one you chose in Play Games or Game Center.
